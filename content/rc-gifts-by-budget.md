We don't print prices on this site, and that's deliberate. RC prices move around constantly, and a stale number is worse than none. So instead of fake "under $50" lists, the tiers below are **relative**, based on how our own guides rank each pick against the others. Check the live price on the Amazon.ca listing before you decide.

What *does* stay true: the price on the listing is not always the price of the gift. A truck with no battery or charger in the box costs more than its sticker, and a toy-grade pick that breaks costs you the whole thing. We've flagged both.

## The real cost of an RC gift

Before comparing tiers, add up the things that aren't always in the box:

- **Battery and charger.** Some listings (like the Traxxas Slash 4X4 on Amazon.ca) don't include them. Others (like the Redcat Volcano EPX) do.
- **A spare battery.** Roughly doubles the fun per outing. Match it to the exact model.
- **Replacement parts.** Hobby-grade cars can be repaired. Toy-grade cars can't.
- **Somewhere to use it.** Boats need water. Planes need open space. A desk drift car needs a desk.

## Modest budget

### Indoors: Hylukon 1:43 Desktop Drift Car

The lowest-risk gift on this site. It's a palm-sized drift car for a desk or coffee table, with a gyro, swappable tires and LED lights. It's not a driveway car, but it's the cheapest honest way to find out whether someone likes RC.

{{product:hylukon-drift-car}}

### Outdoors: DEERC 9309E

Toy-grade, which means it isn't repairable, but it's waterproof and comes with two batteries, so it's a fun "drive something today" gift for a younger teen.

{{product:deerc-9309e}}

### On the water: BEZGAR TX126

A smaller, cheaper boat for learning the fundamentals: one-click self-righting, waterproof, and rated 4.2 stars across 528 Amazon.ca ratings when we wrote our boat guide. They'll need a pond or lake.

{{product:bezgar-tx126}}

### In the air: HAWK'S WORK 2CH RC Glider

A lightweight foam glider built to survive slight crashes. It's an easy, cheap way to fly in a park or schoolyard without needing a big open field. It's a simple glider, not a full trainer plane.

{{product:hawks-work-glider}}

### If they already drive RC: Losi Micro-B 2WD Buggy

A cheap, well-reviewed way into racing-style driving, with an aluminum chassis and real oil-filled shocks. It's a micro buggy meant for cornering, not a beginner's backyard truck.

{{product:losi-micro-b}}

## Middle budget

### Redcat Racing Volcano EPX

A legitimate hobby-grade truck at a noticeably lower price than Traxxas, and it arrives with the battery, charger and radio included, so there's nothing else to buy. The compromise is thinner parts availability. It's our best-value gift for a first hobby-grade RC.

{{product:redcat-volcano-epx}}

### ARRMA Granite

Hobby-grade, rebuildable and upgradeable, for less money than the Slash. Heavier and slower, which makes it forgiving for a newer driver. Check what's included on the listing before you order.

{{product:arrma-granite}}

## Splurge

### Traxxas Slash 4X4

The default first hobby-grade RC: tough, with parts sold almost everywhere. The Amazon.ca listing is the higher-spec "Ultimate" trim and does **not** include a battery or charger, so add those to your total.

{{product:traxxas-slash-4x4}}
