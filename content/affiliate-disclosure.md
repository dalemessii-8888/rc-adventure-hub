RC Adventure Hub is reader-supported. When you click a product link on this site and make a purchase, we may earn a commission from the retailer or brand at no additional cost to you. This is how the site is able to operate.

This is a requirement under U.S. Federal Trade Commission (FTC) guidelines: any relationship between the content you're reading and a financial incentive has to be clearly and conspicuously disclosed. Consider this that disclosure, and consider every "Check Price" or similar button on this site to be a potential affiliate link unless stated otherwise.

## What this means for our recommendations

Being an affiliate site does not mean every product we mention pays us, and it doesn't mean we only recommend products that do. Our policy is:

- We only recommend products we believe are genuinely good for the stated use case, independent of commission rate.
- We do not accept payment from brands in exchange for a positive review or placement in a buying guide.
- Where multiple products meet a recommendation, we say so, rather than steering you toward whichever one pays the highest commission.

## Programs we participate in (or plan to)

- **Amazon Associates** -- an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by linking to Amazon.com.
- **AvantLink** -- an affiliate network through which we may link to brands including Horizon Hobby and AMain Hobbies.
- **ShareASale** -- an affiliate network through which we may link to additional RC hobby brands.
