# Keyword map (for on-page SEO + future content)

No paid keyword-volume tool is connected yet (OpenRush is suggested in
chat, not installed), so this is reasoned long-tail targeting based on
what the direct competitors (mensgear.net, hobbyscoop.com, rccarsguide.com,
cherrypicksreviews.com, funyat.com) are and are not covering, plus what
this site's actual content already argues well. Revisit this once a real
rank-tracking tool is connected.

The competitive gap: every visible competitor is a generic "N Best RC
Cars for Adults" listicle. This site's edge is opinionated, hobby-grade-
specific advice (RTR vs kit, brushed vs brushless, parts availability,
hobby-grade vs toy-grade) that listicles skip. On-page copy should lean
into those exact long-tail phrases, since that's where a small site can
actually rank against bigger domains.

## Content gap ideas for the recurring content task (`automation/README.md`)

Ordered roughly by how directly they extend existing evergreen guides
(cheapest to write well, most linkable from what already exists):

1. "RC Car Parts Availability: Which Brands Actually Have Spares" -- direct
   extension of the parts-availability point already made in the cars guide.
2. "Best RC Cars Under $300 for Adults" -- budget-segmented long-tail, real
   purchase intent, distinct from the existing all-around guide.
3. "Brushed vs. Brushless RC Motors Explained" -- pulls the existing
   brushed/brushless section out into its own evergreen guide, linkable
   from all three vehicle-category articles.
4. "What Is SAFE / AS3X Technology in RC Planes?" -- beginner-protection
   tech is the single biggest differentiator among trainer planes and is
   under-explained by competitors.
5. "RC Boat Maintenance After Saltwater vs Freshwater Use" -- a real gap;
   most boat content assumes freshwater only.

## Notes

- Keep meta titles under ~60 chars and meta descriptions under ~155 --
  Google truncates past that, and the existing frontmatter is already
  disciplined about this; keep new copy to the same limit.
- Don't force an exact-match keyword into a title if it reads unnatural --
  every competitor above already does exact-match titles; a slightly more
  specific, natural title is the differentiator, not another copy of
  "Best RC Cars for Adults."
